# What the best cutters do

Principles the app designs by.

## Drawing
- The cookie is the outer edge of the drawing: smooth and chunky, with no part thinner than about 5 mm.
- The inside is split into a few big rounded areas.
- About 6 to 12 lines, all one width.
- Every line ends on the edge or on another line. This also braces the cutter.
- One or two tiny free marks at most.
- Repeated lines only for one texture, such as hair.

## Cutter
- One piece: the outer blade and the inner lines are joined by crossbars at the top.
- Designed for dough rolled to 5 mm. Thicker dough picks up crossbar marks.
- Flour the cutter before each press.

## Rules in the engine
- Slots narrower than about 3 mm are filled; spikes and necks thinner than about 5 mm are rounded off.
- Lines at least 1 mm wide and 2 mm from the edge; specks under 1.5 mm² are dropped.
- Solid areas over 45 mm² become an outline filled with stripes.
- Holes keep a 5 mm wall of cookie around them.
