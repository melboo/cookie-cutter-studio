Rules every design follows

  • Thin blade tip for a clean cut, thicker above it so the blade stays stiff
  • No slots narrower than 4 mm, since dough sticks in them
  • No spikes or necks thinner than 5 mm, since they'd snap off the cookie
  • Lines at least 1 mm wide and 2 mm from the edge
  • Big solid areas become outlines
  • Crossbars sit at the top, well above 5 mm dough
  • Files come mirrored, so the cookie isn't backwards

Print in PLA or PETG with 0.2 mm layers, rim side down. No supports needed. 3D prints have tiny gaps between layers, so use food-safe filament, keep dough contact short and wash by hand.
